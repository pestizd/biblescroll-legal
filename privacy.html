Bible Scroll

Privacy Policy

Bible Scroll is built to know nothing about you.

Effective 1 November 2026

In short

  • We collect nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your progress, saves and settings stay on your device.
  • With iCloud, they sync through your own private iCloud storage, which we can’t see.
  • Apple handles every purchase. We never see your payment details.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device, and only if you turn them on.

01Who we are

Bible Scroll (“the app”) is published by Stipe Bačić (“we”, “us”). Under data protection law, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the controller responsible for the app. As this policy explains, the app is designed so that we never receive personal data about you. You can reach us at biblescrlsuppmobile@yahoo.com.

02What we collect

Nothing. Bible Scroll has no accounts and no sign-in. It shows no ads and contains no analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. The app does not send information to us or to any server, and we do not sell, share or receive data about you. Reading works fully offline.

03What stays on your device

To remember where you are, the app keeps the following on your device:

  • Your reading progress, streaks, daily goal and the passages you’ve marked as read
  • The passages you save
  • Your settings, such as translation, text size, theme, accent color and reminder time
  • Whether you have Premium (a copy of what Apple’s StoreKit reports, so the app works offline)

This is stored in the app’s private storage on the device (UserDefaults) and in the iOS Keychain. The Keychain copy lets your progress survive if you delete and reinstall the app. Only Bible Scroll can read this data, and it never leaves your device except through iCloud, as described next.

04iCloud sync

If you’re signed into iCloud, the app also keeps a copy of your progress, saves and most settings in your own private iCloud key-value storage, so they stay in step between your iPhone and iPad. This storage belongs to your Apple Account and is provided by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We cannot access it. Nothing is synced when you’re not signed into iCloud.

05Purchases and subscriptions

Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payments, free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple gives the app only a signed record of the purchase (such as which plan you have and when it renews), which the app checks on your device to unlock Premium. Like every developer, we receive sales reports from Apple that are aggregated and don’t identify you.

06Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS asks your permission to show notifications. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device for the time you choose; no server is involved. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

07Sharing and Photos

When you share a passage as an image, the image is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet; where it goes next is up to you. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks permission for the app to add photos to your library. The app can only add images, and only when you ask; it cannot see your photos.

08Children

Bible Scroll is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Anything the app stores stays on the device and in the user’s own iCloud account.

09Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is under your control:

  • On your device: delete the app. This removes its settings, progress and saves from the device.
  • Keychain copy: iOS may keep the small Keychain copy of your progress after the app is deleted, so that reinstalling can restore it. It is removed when the device is erased (Settings › General › Transfer or Reset › Erase All Content and Settings). If you’d like help removing it another way, contact us.
  • iCloud: the synced copy is part of your iCloud account. You can remove app data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage where Bible Scroll is listed, or by turning off iCloud for the app or signing out of iCloud on your devices.

10Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we don’t collect or hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete, but you are always welcome to contact us, and you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

11Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS, including the app sandbox and the device’s encryption. Data in iCloud is protected by Apple.

12Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date at the top, and we’ll point out significant changes in the app’s release notes. The current version is always in the app, in Settings, and on the web page linked from the App Store listing.

13Contact

Questions about privacy, or about this policy? We’re happy to help.

Quick answers

  • Bought Premium before? Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel or change a plan in iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

01Premium on a new iPhone or iPad

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Premium, open Bible Scroll and tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen or in Settings. Your monthly, yearly or Lifetime plan comes back straight away.

02Subscriptions and the free week

New subscribers to the monthly or yearly plan get one week free. Cancel at least 24 hours before the free week ends and you won’t be charged. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. After cancelling, Premium stays until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

If you buy Lifetime while a subscription is running, cancel the subscription yourself so it doesn’t renew.

03Your progress on all your devices

When you’re signed into iCloud, your progress, saves and settings sync between your iPhone and iPad automatically. Make sure iCloud is turned on for Bible Scroll in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Everything also works fully offline.

04Daily reminder

Turn the reminder on in the app’s Settings and pick a time. If it doesn’t appear, check that notifications are allowed for Bible Scroll in Settings › Notifications.

05Found a mistake?

If a passage, an Insight or anything else looks wrong, email us with the reference (for example, Psalm 23:1–6). We read every message.

06Privacy

Bible Scroll collects no personal data. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

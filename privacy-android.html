Bible Scroll for Android

Privacy Policy

Bible Scroll is built to know nothing about you.

Effective 10 November 2026

In short

  • We collect nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your progress, saves and settings stay on your device.
  • If Android backup is on, Google keeps a copy in your own Google Account, which we can’t see.
  • Google Play handles every purchase. We never see your payment details.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device, and only if you turn them on.

01Who we are

Bible Scroll (“the app”) is published by Stipe Bačić (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Android version of the app, distributed through Google Play (package name app.scrollbible). The iPhone and iPad version has its own policy.

Under data protection law, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the controller responsible for the app. As this policy explains, the app is designed so that we never receive personal data about you. You can reach us at biblescrlsuppmobile@yahoo.com.

02What we collect

Nothing. Bible Scroll has no accounts and no sign-in. It shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking. The app’s own code makes no network connections: it does not send information to us or to any server of ours, and we do not sell, share or receive data about you. Reading works fully offline.

The only third-party code in the app is Google’s Play Billing Library, which is how Premium is sold. It talks to Google Play, not to us; section 05 explains what it does.

03What stays on your device

To remember where you are, the app keeps the following on your device:

  • Your reading progress, streaks, daily goal and the passages you’ve marked as read
  • The passages you save
  • Your settings, such as translation, text size, theme, accent color and reminder time
  • Whether you have Premium (a copy of what Google Play reports, so the app works offline)

This is stored in the app’s private storage on the device, which other apps cannot read. It leaves your device only through Android’s own backup, as described next.

04Android backup

If you have turned on backup for your device, Android may include the app’s data (your progress, saves and settings) in the device backup it keeps in your Google Account, so it can be restored when you set up a new phone or reinstall the app. This backup is made by Android, belongs to your Google Account and is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. We cannot access it. Nothing is backed up when device backup is off. The Android app has no other sync between devices.

05Purchases and Google Play

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google processes payments, free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see your name, your Google Account or your payment details.

To do this, the app includes Google’s Play Billing Library. It connects to Google Play on your device to load prices, start a purchase and check whether you have Premium, and it may send Google diagnostic information about how those billing operations performed. That information goes to Google, not to us, and is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google Play gives the app only a record of the purchase (such as which plan you have and its status), which the app checks on your device to unlock Premium. It is not sent to any server of ours. Like every developer, we can see order records in Google Play (such as an order number, the product, the price and the date) and aggregated sales reports, which we use only to answer support and refund requests and to keep our accounts. They don’t include your payment details.

06Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, Android asks your permission to show notifications (on Android 13 and later). The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device for the time you choose; no server is involved. The app sets it again after the device restarts. You can turn it off in the app or in Android’s notification settings at any time.

07Sharing

When you share a passage as an image, the image is created on your device and handed to the Android share sheet; where it goes next is up to you. The app asks for no access to your photos, files, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

08Children

Bible Scroll is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

09Keeping and deleting your data

The app keeps its data on your device for as long as the app is installed. Everything it stores is under your control:

  • On your device: uninstall the app, or open Android Settings › Apps › Bible Scroll › Storage and tap Clear storage. Either removes the app’s settings, progress and saves from the device.
  • Android backup: the backed-up copy is part of your Google Account. You can turn backup off in Android Settings › Google › Backup, or delete a device backup in Google Drive under Storage › Backups.
  • Purchases: your purchase history is kept by Google in your Google Account, not by us.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete, but if you’d like help, contact us.

10Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we don’t collect or hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete, but you are always welcome to contact us, and you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

11Security

Data on your device is protected by Android, including the app sandbox and the device’s encryption. Backups in your Google Account are protected by Google.

12Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date at the top, and we’ll point out significant changes in the app’s release notes. The current version is always on this page, which is linked from the app’s Premium screen and from the Google Play listing.

13Contact

Questions about privacy, or about this policy? We’re happy to help.

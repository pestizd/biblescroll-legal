Bible Scroll is free to download. Premium unlocks more features and is offered as:

Prices may differ by country and are shown in your local currency in the app before you buy. New subscribers may be offered a free trial (currently 7 days). Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription begins and your Apple Account is charged.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically for the same period and price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium stays until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

If you buy Lifetime while you have a subscription, the subscription doesn’t stop on its own. Cancel it in your subscription settings to avoid further charges.

All purchases are processed by Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On a new device signed into the same Apple Account, use Restore Purchases to get Premium back.
